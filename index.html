SATO CORE - DATA & PRIVACY POLICY

1. DATA COLLECTION

We collect the absolute minimum data required to establish the tunneling protocol, verify your account, and process bug reports. We log system interactions, device hardware specifications (including processor and RAM for performance monitoring), and application-level chat data to maintain the integrity of the multiplayer environment. Configuration parameters are stored locally on your device.

2. DISCORD AUTHENTICATION

Discord authentication is used strictly for identity verification. We do not collect or store private profile details from your Discord account. If authorized, our system may automatically join your account to our official community Discord server to manage access roles and server status.

3. USAGE & TUNNELING

Your network traffic is exclusively routed to simulate the multiplayer environment. We monitor application-specific traffic to provide core functionality, such as syncing game state and chat, but we do not track your background applications, monitor your unrelated web browsing history, or scrape personal identifiers outside of the app's immediate scope.

4. THIRD PARTIES

Sato Core does not sell, rent, or lease any analytical data to third parties. All multiplayer server communications are transient and maintained only to ensure connectivity and service stability.

5. SECURITY WARNING

While we route your traffic to simulate a live environment securely, we strongly recommend not transmitting sensitive personal information (like real names, passwords, or financial data) through the proxy tunnel.

6. LIABILITY

By using Sato Core, you acknowledge that you are using a new Satopedia Saori Sato account. Satopedia will not be responsible for any data loss within the Saori Sato games. Since Sato Core functions exclusively within selected applications, our service impact is limited to those specific environments.

7. MULTIPLAYER POLICY

By accessing multiplayer features, you agree to follow these rules. These rules exist to keep multiplayer fair, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

FAIR PLAY

  • Do not use cheats, unauthorized third-party software, or exploits to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Do not intentionally abuse bugs or glitches that negatively affect other players.
  • Do not manipulate game data or network traffic to gain an unfair advantage.

PLAYER BEHAVIOR

  • Do not harass, threaten, or repeatedly target other players.
  • Do not spam or intentionally disrupt multiplayer sessions.
  • Do not impersonate developers, moderators, or other players.

EXPLOITS AND BUGS

  • If you discover a serious exploit, please report it to the Satopedia Discord support server rather than deliberately abusing it.
  • The developers may temporarily restrict access to multiplayer features while investigating exploits or technical issues.

MODERATION

  • We may investigate reports of rule violations.
  • Depending on the severity of a violation, we may issue warnings, remove players from sessions, temporarily suspend multiplayer access, or permanently restrict access.
  • False reports or attempts to manipulate moderation systems may themselves be treated as violations. Please report all rule violations in the Discord server.

GAME MODIFICATIONS

  • Modifications that only affect your local game may be permitted where explicitly supported.
  • Modifications that provide an unfair multiplayer advantage or interfere with other players or the servers are not permitted.

SERVER INTEGRITY

  • Do not intentionally overload, disrupt, attack, or interfere with multiplayer servers.
  • Do not attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts, servers, or administrative systems.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

These rules may be updated when necessary. Significant changes will be communicated to players where appropriate.

8. GLOBAL SHUTDOWN

By selecting 'I Agree', you confirm that you acknowledge that the multiplayer features may be shut down globally for all players if a serious threat for the developers is met to ensure safety of all players. These shutdowns are almost never permanent, and the probability of them happening is very low. However, they may still happen.

9. ACCEPTANCE

By selecting 'I Agree', you confirm that you have read and agree to follow this Multiplayer Policy.
